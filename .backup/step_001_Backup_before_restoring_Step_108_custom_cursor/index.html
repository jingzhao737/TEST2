Color Series · 2024–2026

Chromatic Flux

A deep exploration of fluid color fields, shifting pigments, and visual resonance. This series captures the transition of organic dyes under refractive light, creating a dynamic dialogue between material limits and chromatic depth.

Generative Series · 2025

The Shape of Silence

Generative digital paintings exploring quiet geometry, mathematical emptiness, and subtle noise. Using custom pixel-sorting and noise algorithms, each frame acts as a visual meditation on modern digital friction.

Installation · 2023–Present

Neon Liturgy

Site-specific light art installations utilizing glass neon tubes, structural steel, and reflective mirrors. A sacred architecture built with gas and electricity, celebrating the tension between historic ritual and industrial waste.

Text Art

DO NOT GO GENTLE

DYLAN THOMAS

c. 1947

[ SYS.MODE // PROXIMITY_LIGHT ]

[ LIGHT.VAL // 0.15 - 1.00 ]

[ DIST.TRIG // 150px_WAVE ]

Do not go gentle into that good night,

Old age should burn and rave at close of day;

Rage, rage against the dying of the light.

Though wise men at their end know dark is right,

Because their words had forked no lightning they

Do not go gentle into that good night.

Good men, the last wave by, crying how bright

Their frail deeds might have danced in a green bay,

Rage, rage against the dying of the light.

Wild men who caught and sang the sun in flight,

And learn, too late, they grieved it on its way,

Do not go gentle into that good night.

Grave men, near death, who see with blinding sight

Blind eyes could blaze like meteors and be gay,

Rage, rage against the dying of the light.

And you, my father, there on the sad height,

Curse, bless, me now with your fierce tears, I pray.

Do not go gentle into that good night.

Rage, rage against the dying of the light.

About the Artist

I’m a visual artist working at the intersection of traditional craft and digital experimentation—where paint meets pixels.

My work spans oil painting, digital art, sculpture, and installation. I don’t believe in medium boundaries—the idea dictates the tools, not the other way around. Every piece is a conversation between intention and accident.

Based in Shanghai, exhibiting internationally. My work has appeared in galleries across Asia and Europe, and in private collections worldwide.
